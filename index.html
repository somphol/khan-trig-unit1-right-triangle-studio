Reciprocal Ratios & Duality

Cosecant, secant and cotangent are not new magic — they are the primary ratios flipped upside down.

Goal: see the flip, not a new rule

csc θ is just 1 ÷ sin θ — same triangle, flipped fraction.

⇄
sec θ hypadj 1.2208

sec θ is just 1 ÷ cos θ. At 90° the adjacent side collapses, so sec is undefined.

⇄

tan θ and cot θ are reciprocals. At 90° the adjacent side is 0, so both blow up.
